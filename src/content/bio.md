I am a Machine Learning Algorithm Engineer at OPPO Research Institute. My work centers on agentic mobile operation, collaborative intelligence, and efficient learning systems.

I am interested in turning research ideas into usable infrastructure—especially systems that can perceive interfaces, reason over long-horizon tasks, collaborate across agents, and learn efficiently across devices.

My research interests include **GUI agents**, **multi-agent collaboration**, **federated learning**, **edge-cloud computing**, and **recommendation systems**.
